The bot
A proprietary crypto trend-following system. Every trade has a stop loss set at entry and risks 1% of equity. No profit targets: winners are allowed to run.

Profits come from a few large trades, so streaks of small losses are normal. Public goal: reach 2027-04-30 with positive total R and a maximum drawdown below 33%.
Confidence meter
A backtest is like a positive medical test: encouraging, but not proof. Every live trade is new, independent evidence. After each closed trade the bot updates the probability that its edge is real (Bayes' theorem), comparing live results with two scenarios: a real edge (+0.4R per trade) and no edge (−0.1R, just fees). It started at 50%. If it falls below 10%, the bot will publicly declare that it failed.
Seal
Every record carries a sha256 seal chained to the previous one: deleting or editing a loss breaks every seal after it. The chain is checked on every update, and the public log of all trades is in operacoes.jsonl.
